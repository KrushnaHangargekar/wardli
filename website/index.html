Capabilities

Built for everyday style clarity.

No cluttered social feeds, no algorithmic noise. Just your genuine wardrobe, smart deterministic rules, and complete ownership.

Complete Digital Wardrobe & Accessories

Catalog Tops, Bottoms, Footwear, and all your Accessories — watches, belts, sunglasses, jewelry, bags, and caps. Create custom categories on demand.

  • Availability statuses: Clean, Worn, In Laundry, Reserved
  • Color harmony tags and purchase cost tracking
  • Private owner-level isolation for all records and photos

One-Tap Rule-Based Suggestions

Generate up to three smart outfit suggestions tailored for College, Office, Casual, Party, Formal, or Travel without any machine learning or third-party APIs.

  • Never suggests unavailable, worn, or in-laundry clothes
  • Single-item swap: replace any piece without rebuilding the outfit
  • Configurable repeat-avoidance window (3 to 30 days)

Exact Outfit History & Wear Tracking

Tap "I Wore This" to freeze an immutable wear snapshot. View history grouped by Day, Week, and Month, or manually record past outfits.

  • Snapshot preservation: past history stays safe even if items are archived
  • Leaderboard of most-worn and least-worn items
  • Search history by date range or occasion

Weekly Planner & Laundry Alerts

Plan your looks for the coming week. Planned outfits remain strictly separated from history and do not increment wear counts until confirmed.

  • Real-time alert warnings if a planned item is In Laundry or Reserved
  • One-tap "Mark as Worn" converts plans to verified history
  • Clean week-at-a-glance calendar view
Integrity First

100% Free Core Features. Zero AI Gimmicks.

Wardli uses deterministic algorithms for color harmony, category matching, and repetition scoring. We never send your wardrobe photos or records to third-party AI models. Every data record is isolated to your private account.
